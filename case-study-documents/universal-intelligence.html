Product structure

Team enablement

Make context easier to join

  • Documented behavior and decisions so new employees have a practical starting point.

What comes next

Keep the repository alive through the feature workflow.

Its usefulness depends on goals, decisions, and delivery context staying current. The next step is to make those updates part of normal feature work and test whether new teammates can find the context they need without a separate handoff.
