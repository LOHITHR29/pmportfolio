spotify case study 03 / 16

(research)

How might we keep India’s casual listeners coming back to Spotify each week?

Research context

Casual free-tier listeners open Spotify a few times after install, then quietly stop. We want to understand why the second-month curve flattens, and whether discovery is the lever.

Hypotheses

  • Discovery surfaces too few fresh, region-relevant tracks after the first 2 — 3 sessions.
  • Free-tier constraints (ads, shuffle-only, no skips) feel punitive vs JioSaavn / Wynk Music.
  • Indian regional / Hindi catalogue is under-curated for the segment’s taste.

1:1 user-interview questionnaire

  • Walk me through your day — when do you listen to music? How often?
  • Which apps do you use today? What do you love and what frustrates you about each?
  • Tell me about the last time you opened Spotify. What did you do, what did you listen to, did you finish?
  • How do you discover new music? Recommendations, friends, Reels, radio, somewhere else?
  • What would make you reach for Spotify first instead of YouTube or JioSaavn?
  • What would make you happy to pay ₹X / month for an audio app? Anchor on your current spend.
spotify case study 04 / 16

(insights)

User insights from 12 interviews.

Behaviour

  • 18 — 30 commuters / students. 30 — 45 min sessions, 2 — 3 days a week.
  • Listening usually parallel to another task — commute, work, gym, cooking.
  • Already saturated with TikTok / Reels audio + YouTube Shorts. Spotify is round-3 in the consideration set.
  • Most willing to pay ₹99 — ₹149 / month if the catalogue + experience compounds week on week.

Pain points (Spotify Free)

  • Daily Mixes feel repetitive after 2 — 3 weeks; same 50 tracks loop.
  • Forced shuffle on free tier breaks listening flow, especially during workouts and commute.
  • Hindi + regional catalogue feels thin vs JioSaavn. Regional discovery is buried.
  • Ads cut in at the worst moment — the high-energy second song of a session.

Expectations

  • A discovery surface that surfaces fresh, region-relevant tracks each session.
  • More agency on the free tier — at least N skips per day, no shuffle-only restriction on user-built playlists.
  • A clearer path from I-like-this-song → more-like-this without leaving the player.
  • Social cues — what friends are listening to right now, not just retro Wrapped recaps.

spotify case study 09 / 16

(problem statement)

What is the true problem?

Casual Indian free-tier users churn because Spotify’s discovery surface keeps showing the same global-leaning Daily Mix while the listener’s actual taste — Hindi, regional, mood-led — sits underneath three taps and feels under-curated.

Free-tier constraints (forced shuffle, tight skip cap, ad timing) compound the frustration before the user has stayed long enough to feel taste compounding.

Why now?

JioSaavn and Wynk have caught up on regional curation; YouTube Music is winning the discovery → save loop in India; Reels has become the new audio-discovery tool. Free-tier weekly retention in India has slowed Q / Q. The window to fix it is shrinking.

For users

A discovery surface that learns Indian taste fast and surfaces fresh region-relevant tracks every session — not the same loop after two weeks.

For business

Even a +0.6 lift in sessions / user / week across the ~7 mn segment compounds into meaningful Premium India conversion. Internal estimate ≈ ~$28 mn ARR uplift at steady state*.

*7 mn × 8 % conversion lift × ₹49/mo × 12 ÷ ₹84/USD ≈ ~$28 mn

(RICE)

Solution prioritisation — RICE on four candidate bets.

Solution Reach Impact Confidence Effort Priority
Regional Discovery Hub — a dedicated, ML-curated India surface (Hindi / Punjabi / regional) that learns from in-session signals. H H H M 1
Free-tier Daily Skips allowance + ad-pacing rework (more agency, fewer flow breakers). H M M L 2
Mood-and-moment playlists that adapt to time of day + activity (commute, gym, focus). H M L/M M 3
Friends’ Now-Playing ribbon — surface what your contacts are listening to, right now, not retro Wrapped. M M M M 4

For India’s casual-listener segment, regional taste is the shared deficit — and the bet that compounds across every other surface (search, daily mixes, autoplay, ad pacing). It’s the highest-leverage starting point.

spotify case study 11 / 16

(chosen solution)

/regional discovery hub.

A first-class India surface inside Spotify Free — Hindi, Punjabi, and regional content curated by both editorial and in-session signals. Three surfaces work together:

Discover · Today

A live, regional “For You” shelf

Refreshes daily with ~20 new tracks based on the user’s last 7-day skip / save / replay pattern. Weighted toward Indian regional artists.

Mood-and-moment

Time-aware curation

Commute, gym, study, late-night, festival drives. Each with a regional-first edit.

Friends ribbon

Live "Now Playing"

See what your contacts on Spotify are listening to right now (opt-in). The social proof Reels has, brought back home to Spotify.

spotify case study 12 / 16

(user stories · listener side)

User stories — the casual listener seeking better discovery.

Discover · Today

  • As a user, I should land on a Today shelf above my Daily Mixes that surfaces 15 — 25 fresh, region-relevant tracks each morning.
  • As a user, I should see why a track was picked for me (e.g. “based on your saves last week”).
  • As a user, I should be able to swipe-dismiss a track and have the model down-weight that artist for a week.
  • As a user, my first save / replay in a session should reshape what I see for the rest of the week.

Mood-and-moment

  • As a user, I should see playlists that match my time of day and likely activity (commute, gym, focus, late night).
  • As a user, each mood playlist should feel India-first — Hindi / regional weighted, not the global default.
  • As a user, I should be able to mark a mood as “not for me” without it affecting my Daily Mix taste graph.
spotify case study 13 / 16

(user stories · social + free-tier pacing)

User stories — friends ribbon + free-tier rework.

Friends ribbon

  • As a user, I should see a slim ribbon of contacts who are listening on Spotify right now (opt-in privacy default off).
  • As a user, I should be able to tap a friend and join their session OR jump to the song they’re on.
  • As a user, I should be able to react with a single emoji on what they’re listening to — soft, low-friction social.

Free-tier pacing (V2)

  • As a user, I should get an additional “daily skips” allowance once I hit certain engagement signals (saves, shares).
  • As a user, ads should not interrupt the second track of a fresh session — pace ads after the third or fourth track instead.
  • As a user, my own playlists should play in order on free tier (drop the global shuffle-only constraint).
spotify case study 14 / 16

(success metrics)

How we’ll know the bet worked.

North-Star (NSM)

  • Sessions / user / week (free) — target 3.1 → 4.5+ in the casual-listener cohort.
  • 30-day return rate for new free users in India (cohort vs control).

Adoption · Discovery (L1)

  • Discover · Today CTR on shelf items.
  • Save rate within Discover · Today vs Daily Mix baseline.
  • % sessions that reach the Discover · Today shelf.

Retention (L1)

  • D7 and D30 retention for new India free users.
  • Drop-off rate within session post first ad break.
  • Skip rate on free tier — direction matters more than absolute.

Revenue + Check (L2)

  • Free → Premium conversion in India for the cohort.
  • Ad CPM × ad-impression on free tier (rebalance, don’t collapse).
  • Customer Sat / NPS on the free-tier discovery experience.
  • Friends ribbon opt-in % — social health check.
